About Us

Founded by Giovanni Ardila, a hospitality professional with 15 years of hands-on experience in the catering and events industry, Ardila Staffing LLC was created with a simple mission: to connect businesses with experienced hospitality professionals who understand the demands of every event.

Throughout his career, Giovanni has worked in a variety of hospitality roles, including server, bartender, catering captain, and pantry support. This firsthand experience provides a unique understanding of what businesses need to deliver successful events and exceptional guest experiences.

Ardila Staffing LLC specializes in connecting catering companies, event teams, and hospitality businesses with reliable 1099 independent contractors for a wide range of events, including corporate functions, weddings, galas, private events, and special occasions.

Our goal is to provide flexible staffing support that helps businesses stay prepared while giving skilled hospitality professionals opportunities to work in the events they enjoy.

  • Experienced hospitality professionals
  • Staffing support for corporate events, weddings, galas, and private functions
  • Flexible 1099 independent contractor opportunities
  • Industry knowledge built from real catering experience
